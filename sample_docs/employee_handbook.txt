Sample Employee Handbook

Annual leave: Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave each calendar year. Leave requests should be submitted to a manager at least two weeks in advance.

Remote work: Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval. Some roles may require additional on-site attendance.

Public holidays: The office is closed on official public holidays. If a team must provide holiday coverage, the manager will arrange a rotating schedule.

Expenses: Work-related expenses must be submitted with receipts within 30 days of purchase. Expenses above $500 require written manager approval before purchase.

Equipment: The company provides a laptop for work purposes. Employees should contact IT support to report lost or damaged equipment.
